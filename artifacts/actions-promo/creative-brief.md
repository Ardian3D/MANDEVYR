# MANDEVYR — A move on your terms

Status: approved by the user; produced as a 40-second standalone HTML film with narration and original sound design.

## Concept

Candidates: journey inside the UI; one feature demonstrated fully; a key as a metaphor for wallet control.

Selected: journey inside the UI, because the new Actions experience should be seen in use.

Structural fingerprint: UI journey · five connected beats · Actions control, vault selector, EURC composer, contract evidence, product/brand · opens inside an Actions detail · closes by pulling back from the product to MANDEVYR.

## Style

- 40 seconds, 1920 × 1080, 16:9. English female narration if credits allow.
- Feeling: calm, precise, premium.
- Brand mint #cce8b5, dark green #0a100a, light green #e2ebdb, from the current Actions UI palette.
- Display: editorial italic serif; UI: the product's Manrope. No highlighted-word pill. Short display phrases have no automatic final punctuation.

- Background: dark green gradient plus moving soft illumination; light brand field revealed as the product pulls away at the end.
- Signature motion: camera moves toward a choice, then follows the resulting UI state. Components assemble and respond; no sequence of faded slides.
- Main moment: contract evidence rises from its panel after EURC is selected.
- Transitions: connected camera moves inside one product world; depth reveal into the interface; product pullback opens the light ending.
- Original brand and curator assets only; no generated people or stock footage required.
